Description

This is one of the first Professional Rolex watches, a deep-sea diving watch that has become the archetype of this category. The Oyster Perpetual Submariner watch, released in 1953, was the world's first diving wristwatch with water resistance up to 100 meters. The modern versions of the Submariner and Submariner Date watches, which represent the quintessence of advanced technologies in watchmaking, are distinguished by their characteristics that guarantee their reliability and chronometric accuracy. The Submariner watches accompany not only brave explorers of the ocean depths, but also adventurers in other equally fascinating areas. Thanks to its many fans, this iconic model has gone beyond its original purpose and gained worldwide recognition.

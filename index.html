Frequently Asked Questions

  • How does the pawn shop work?

    A pawn shop offers short-term loans using personal items as collateral. You bring in an item of value, such as jewelry or electronics, and the pawn shop assesses its worth. Based on this value, they offera loan amount. If you agree, you receive the money and a ticket for reclaiming your item. You mustrepay the loan, including interest, within the agreed time. If you can't repay, the pawn shop keeps the item, but your credit score isn’t affected. Alternatively, you can sell items outright without taking a loan.
